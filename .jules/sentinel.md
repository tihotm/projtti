## 2024-05-18 - Added Security Policy to Documentation-Only Repository
**Vulnerability:** Lack of a standard vulnerability reporting mechanism.
**Learning:** For repositories that consist purely of documentation, audits, or architectural decisions, standard code-level vulnerabilities might not apply. However, a security policy is still crucial for responsible disclosure of concerns regarding the documented architecture or decisions.
**Prevention:** Always include a `SECURITY.md` file even for documentation-focused projects to provide a clear channel for security communications.
